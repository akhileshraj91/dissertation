\section*{Abstract}
Connecting Internet-of-things devices and edge computing to high-performance computing
(HPC) systems introduces new challenges to capture the transformative potential of
the computing continuum. User workflows in this edge-to-HPC continuum might require
dynamic resource provisioning and control at the edge to take advantage of artificial
intelligence (AI) inference at the data source (AI@Edge), real-time experiment
steering, and model retraining through HPC systems. In this chapter we discuss
the case of an edge-to-HPC workflow from beamline science and illustrate how
such a workflow can benefit from resource arbitration and reconfiguration. From
this driving example we highlight our work toward the design of a framework for
AI@Edge resource management and demonstrate the benefits of our approach on an
initial experiment, showcasing our ability to observe and adapt resources to meet
a target application throughput.
