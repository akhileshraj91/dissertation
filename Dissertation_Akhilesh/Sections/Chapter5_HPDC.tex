\section*{Abstract}
As the compute intensity of modern AI/ML and high performance computing (HPC) workloads
grows, so does their energy consumption, making energy efficiency a major
sustainability challenge. Although substantial prior research has dealt with the
design of more energy-efficient hardware or more efficient algorithms, not much
work exists on improving the energy efficiency of already deployed applications.
In this chapter, we present an offline reinforcement learning (RL) approach for
application-agnostic power control of HPC compute nodes. The controller is trained
from pre-collected execution traces, capturing hardware performance counter data
alongside power consumption measurements, and is deployed without retraining on
diverse test workloads. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work to
apply offline RL for dynamic power regulation in HPC/data center environments.
We demonstrate significant energy savings while maintaining application performance
across a range of memory-bandwidth-bound and compute-bound benchmarks.
